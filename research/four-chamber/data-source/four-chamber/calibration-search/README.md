# Lead II calibration search

The final displayed model is rebuilt by `scripts/build_four_chamber_model.py`
from the explicit parameters in `../lead-ii-calibration.json`, the pooled human
AP template, and the anatomical and torso inputs documented in the main methods.
The optimizer is **not** required during that deterministic rebuild.

This directory additionally retains the exploration code and its numerical
results, including alternatives that were rejected. These searches calibrated a
reduced forward model to the measured PTB healthy-control record
`patient104/s0306lre`. The reference is a fitting target, not an independent
validation subject. Electrode lead II was the fitting objective; other leads are
reported separately and were not optimized.

## Search sequence

1. `explore_fit.py`: four regional delivery times and a shared travel-speed scale.
   Differential evolution, seed 731, followed by bounded Nelder–Mead. The best
   unconstrained chronology was rejected despite its lower error.
2. `fit_chronology.py`: constrain the three LV regions to approximately
   simultaneous onset and the RV to a short subsequent delay; additionally fit
   a common ventricular source-conductivity scale.
3. `fit_atria.py` and `fit_atria_extended.py`: fit sinus onset, LA entry delay,
   isotropic atrial travel speed, and effective atrial source conductivity. The
   second version uses the reconstructed appendage and requires LA entry at
   least 20 ms after sinus onset.
4. `fit_distributed.py`: independently time the original 12 delivery sites,
   retaining bounded LV and RV onset intervals. Three deterministic starting
   points and bounded least squares are used.
5. `fit_fast_endocardium.py`: independently increase endocardial propagation
   while retaining slower myocardial propagation. This did not improve the
   selected lead II morphology.
6. `fit_basal_delivery.py`: add three modeled basal RV delivery sites, then fit
   their delayed arrival together with the original 12 sites. The objective
   includes the voltage discrepancy, the first-derivative discrepancy, and a
   penalty on renewed upward motion after the main R wave. **These terms select
   activation parameters; they do not filter or edit the calculated ECG.**
7. `fit_recovery.py`, `fit_warped_recovery.py`, and `fit_pooled_recovery.py`:
   compare single-pixel optical, published TT06 ionic, adapted TT06, smooth
   phenomenological, and pooled human optical AP families. Fit only the APD
   field when comparing the fixed data-derived pooled families. The large TT06
   APD50 distortion was rejected as a default.

Each search retains all signed physical source weights. To make many parameter
trials practical, source weights are deposited bilinearly into activation-time
and APD bins, then convolved with membrane APs. Typical search bins are 0.5 ms
for activation and 2–4 ms for APD. Verification uses finer bins or direct
all-source evaluation. No reference ECG samples are inserted into an output
waveform, and no P/QRS/T basis functions or post-hoc ECG filters are used.

## Historical replay inputs

The initial search used the source operator and AP templates of published commit
`e732cb67fb0cafb795dd9a7f0cd76844dae793e5`. The final selected model later adds the
appendage and dense source-field evaluation. Those are distinct experimental
inputs; using a later calibrated operator without undoing its conductivities
would change the experiment.

To replay the historical searches, first rebuild that baseline revision and copy
its `data-source/four-chamber/operators-cache.npz` into a working directory as
`baseline-operators.npz`. Copy the provided compact
`baseline-heart-model-inputs.json` into the same directory as
`baseline-heart-model.json`. The compact file contains exactly the AP templates
and ECG arrays consumed by the searches. Set `ECG_CALIBRATION_WORKDIR` to that
directory. The unchanged original Rodero input is read from the main source
directory; generated root-distance caches are kept in the working directory.

The appendage atrial fit additionally uses `ECG_EXTENDED_OPERATOR`, a source
operator assembled on the extended anatomy at the **baseline** conductivities
0.20/0.05 S/m for ventricles and 0.11 S/m for atria, with baseline propagation
0.70/0.30 mm/ms, 2.0 mm/ms endocardially, and 1.0 mm/ms in atria. The source
refinement experiment uses `ECG_BASELINE_MIDPOINT_OPERATOR`, generated by
`scripts/refine_source_field.py` on the unextended baseline anatomy.

The large generated operator caches are not redistributed in this compact
source package. Their assembly, source data, parameters, and consistency checks
are included in the main rebuild. The JSON results here preserve the exact
historical parameter choices and traces. Running a new fit is an exploration,
not a claim that a single optimizer trajectory uniquely identifies physiology.

## Timing and amplitude conventions

The optimization used a fixed reference R anchor of 231 ms. The final lesson
translates every atrial and ventricular onset and the reference by the same
30 ms to leave a visible resting interval. This changes no interval, shape, or
amplitude. Reports include both fixed-clock and R-aligned comparisons in raw mV.

The source conductivities are explicitly fitted effective parameters. In this
graph/prescribed-AP model they are independent of the propagation-speed
parameters. This is not a coupled ionic tissue PDE or a measured conductivity
estimate. The 15 delivery sites are a calibrated Purkinje-delivery surrogate,
not junctions measured in the anatomy or ECG donor.
